Ocean Food — Brand Identity & Packaging Solution

Discover a complete food brand built around one teal wave — a logo, a colour system and a packaging range designed for a modern, trustworthy shelf presence.

Enjoy smooth consistency across every pack: spices, cooking pastes, vinegar and pink salt all carry the same mark, the same colours and the same voice, so a customer recognises Ocean Food from across the store.

Built with a clean, minimal and honest design language, the Ocean Food identity improves shelf visibility, strengthens trust and gives the brand a professional presence in every market it enters.
