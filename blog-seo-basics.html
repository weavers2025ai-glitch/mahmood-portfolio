SEO Basics: How Google Finds Your Website

By Syed Mahmood Shah • 30 Oct 2026 • Weavers Creation

When someone searches "best bakery in Peshawar" or "electronics store near me", Google chooses a handful of winners. SEO - search engine optimisation - is simply the craft of deserving to be one of them.

Google reads before it ranks

Google’s robots crawl your pages and try to understand them. Clear page titles, one main heading per page, honest descriptions and text that actually says what you sell - this is how a website introduces itself. Pages stuffed with repeated keywords read like a pushy salesman and get treated like one.

The three foundations

Technical: your site loads fast, works on phones and has no broken pages. Content: your pages answer real questions better than rivals do. Reputation: other sites, directories and your Google Business Profile mention and link to you. Most small businesses win by simply being solid on all three while competitors ignore two.

Local SEO is the small business superpower

For a local business, your Google Business Profile may matter more than your website. Correct address, real photos, current hours and genuine reviews put you on the map - literally - when nearby customers search. It is free, and it is neglected by almost everyone.

Quick Tips

  • Write page titles like newspaper headlines: clear, specific, honest.
  • Claim and complete your Google Business Profile today.
  • Ask happy customers for Google reviews - politely, every time.
  • Publish one useful page a month; consistency compounds.

SEO is not a trick played on Google. It is a reputation built in public, one clear page at a time.
